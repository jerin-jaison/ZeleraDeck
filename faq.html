Questions before putting your business online?
Good. You should have them.

From website costs and domain renewals to Google ranking realities, WhatsApp enquiries, and our pay-when-you-get-sales philosophy — here is everything you should know.

Does my small business really need a website in 2026?
Yes. A website gives your business an official digital home where customers can find your services, location, pricing, and contact details 24/7 without needing to scroll through social feeds. While social platforms attract attention, a website builds immediate credibility and converts searchers into paying customers.
Is a website necessary if I already have an Instagram page?
Instagram is great for visual discovery, but it cannot replace a dedicated business website. Instagram limits your layout, buries contact info, doesn't index well on Google Search for local intent, and algorithm changes can reduce your reach overnight. A website is your own digital property that ranks on Google and handles customer enquiries directly.
Is a website necessary if I already use WhatsApp for business?
WhatsApp is a messaging tool, not a discovery platform. New customers cannot find your WhatsApp number unless they discover your business elsewhere first. A website acts as the top of your funnel—attracting Google searchers and turning them into WhatsApp messages with one click.
Do local shops and neighborhood businesses need websites?
Definitely. Modern local customers search Google for 'best bakery near me', 'salon in Panampilly Nagar', or 'tuition center near me' before stepping out. A lightweight local website ensures your shop appears in local search results and gives customers instant answers about your location, hours, and offerings.
Does a small salon or beauty clinic need a website?
Yes. A salon website displays your service menu, pricing, before-and-after portfolios, customer reviews, and direct booking links. It answers 'How much for haircut?' before clients even call, saving time and building trust.
Does a restaurant or cafe need a website?
Yes. Customers want to view your menu, check opening hours, see food photos, find your address on Google Maps, and place direct WhatsApp orders without paying high delivery app commission fees.
Does a tuition centre or coaching institute need a website?
Yes. Parents search for verified coaching centers with course details, teacher credentials, batch timings, fee structures, and student achievements. A website gives your institute legitimate academic authority.
Does a freelancer or independent professional need a website?
Yes. As a freelancer, your website is your digital resume and portfolio. It allows clients to review your past work, read testimonials, understand your pricing packages, and hire you directly.
Is a website worth the investment for a local Indian small business?
Yes. A professional website costs less than a single monthly local advertisement and works for you 365 days a year. It elevates your business above competitors who rely solely on word-of-mouth or social pages.
What is the primary benefit of having a website for a small business?
Credibility and discovery. When potential customers search for services in your area, a website proves your business is legitimate, active, and easy to reach.
Can a website make my small business look more professional?
Instantly. Having your own custom domain signals to customers and suppliers that you take your business seriously.
Why should a traditional offline business establish a web presence?
Because consumer habits have shifted. Even when buying offline, over 80% of shoppers start their research online. If you aren't visible online, you're giving away customers to nearby competitors who are.
What essential details should a small business put on its website?
Keep it clear and simple: What you do, who you serve, key products/services, clear pricing, location map, photos/proof of work, and direct action buttons (WhatsApp, Phone Call, Directions).
Can a single-page website be enough for a small business?
Yes! A clean, well-structured single-page website often converts better than a bloated 10-page site because visitors get all essential answers on one screen without getting lost.

Customers searching on Google or asking AI assistants will naturally see and contact your competitor first. A website ensures you capture your share of local demand.
